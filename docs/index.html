OLIST — 99K BRAZILIAN E-COMMERCE ORDERS

8% of orders arrive late, and those orders produce 55% of every 1–2 star review. But the sharper finding is what the chart shows: Amazonas takes 26.4 days — the slowest state in Brazil — and still scores 4.10, while Alagoas delivers faster and scores 3.80. The difference is that Amazonas keeps its promise and Alagoas does not. Customers do not punish slow delivery. They punish a broken date. The operational fix is cheaper than it looks: quote realistic windows instead of racing the truck. Built as a reproducible pipeline over 9 related tables and 1.5M rows, with a documented data-quality audit.

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY OF SALES PERFORMANCE

Orders discounted above 20% are 14% of the book. They bill $363K and lose $135K — dropping them alone would lift total profit by 47%. The break is clean: below 20% every discount tier is profitable, and above it not a single one is. At 80% off, the store loses $1.80 for every dollar it takes in. A second finding sits underneath: the West region returns 15.3% of its lines against 4–5% everywhere else.

The share fell 56.3% in the ten weeks to March 2020, then rose 322% off that low to its April 2024 high. The headline number for the period — up 70.7% — hides both. TABLEAU PROJECT
AIRBNB REVENUES

61.8% of the city’s 36,561 listings belong to hosts running more than one property, and 42 hosts alone control 3,948 of them. Anyone listing an apartment in Buenos Aires is competing with property managers, not with neighbours. Palermo and Recoleta hold 46.7% of all supply between them; a four-guest apartment is the efficient point in the market, earning 43% more per night than a two-guest one at the lowest price per guest in the city; and Superhost status buys volume rather than rate — five times the reviews for a price premium under 6%. A caveat surfaced in the build: the source calendar file stops at 1,048,575 rows, one short of Excel’s limit, so every occupancy figure rests on 7.9% of the market.

Covid 19 Data

Among countries past a million cases, reported case fatality runs from 4.88% in Peru to 0.07% in Singapore — a 70-fold spread. No virus varies that much. What the number is really measuring is health-system capacity, age structure and, above all, how each country counted. Stating that caveat is part of the finding, not a footnote to it. Cyprus tops recorded infection at 77.1% of its population.

Wild Bike Store

Bikes are 72.5% of revenue at a 33.2% margin. Accessories are 17.7% of revenue at 58.6% — and move 29 times the units. The revenue and the margin are in different places: the bike is the hook, the accessory is the business. By country, Australia is the second-largest market on the worst margin (31.8%) while Canada is fifth on the best (46.8%). And the segmentation that was supposed to matter, gender, separates nothing at all: 50.8% against 49.2%, at 38.0% and 37.5% margin. Reporting that honestly is worth more than forcing a difference out of 1.3 points.
